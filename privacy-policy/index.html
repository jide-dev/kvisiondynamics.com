Privacy Policy

LadderQuest: Caveman Climb · Effective date: 17 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KVision Dynamics (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you play LadderQuest: Caveman Climb (the “Game”) on iOS, Android, and Steam. By playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email, or other directly identifying personal information to play. The Game collects the following automatically:

  • Device & technical data — device model, OS version, language/region, app version, and a pseudonymous app/device identifier used to count unique players and diagnose issues.
  • Gameplay & usage analytics — events such as game sessions, level start/complete, in-game progress and economy actions, and errors, used to understand how the Game is played and to improve it.
  • Crash & performance data — diagnostic logs and device state captured when the Game crashes or misbehaves.
  • Advertising data — your device’s advertising identifier and limited ad-interaction data, used to deliver and measure ads (see §4–5).
  • Purchase data — when you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by the platform store (Apple, Google, or Steam). We receive a record that a purchase occurred to grant your items; we do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game, its features and difficulty.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and monitor performance and stability.
  • To deliver, personalize (where permitted), and measure advertising.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and technical issues.

3. Legal bases (EU/UK)

Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, we process data on the basis of our legitimate interests (operating and improving the Game, security), the performance of a contract (delivering purchases), and your consent (analytics and personalized advertising, where consent is required).

4. Third-party services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (usage analytics and crash reporting) — Firebase Privacy / Google Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising via Google AdMob and any mediated ad networks — AdMob & partner policies.
  • In-app purchases processed by the Apple App Store, Google Play, and Steam — Apple / Google / Valve/Steam.

5. Advertising & your choices

The Game may show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch. On iOS, we request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency before any tracking for personalized advertising; if you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads. On Android and other platforms, you can opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Where required, we present a consent choice for advertising and analytics.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We do not sell personal information for money.

8. Data retention

We retain analytics and diagnostic data for as long as needed for the purposes described above and as required by our service providers’ default retention periods, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

9. Data security & international transfers

We and our service providers use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country, including the United States, with appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, through an in-Game notice.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: support@kvisiondynamics.com.
